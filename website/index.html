Welcome

Welcome to HPComm.org. HPComm and HPComm38 are free programs which allow you to transfer data between a PC, and HP graphing calculators.

HPComm and HPComm38 were developed by James Bergamin and Mitch Davis.

HPComm and HPComm38 are also distributed by Hewlett Packard, as the official software in the "HP Graphing Calculator PC Connectivity Kit".

We produce two programs. HPComm is used for communicating with HP48 and HP49G calculators, and HPComm38 is used for communicating with the HP38G, HP39G and HP40G calculators. The latest released version of HPComm is HPComm 3.0r4. The latest version of HPComm38 is 1.0r4. You can visit the changes page to find what what is new and fixed. Or, you can visit the development page and beta-testing page for more information.

HPComm and HPComm38 are Open Source software programs, and are licensed under the GNU General Public License. As such, you are entitled to receive a copy of the source code. If you'd like a copy, or would like to help with the development of HPComm or HPComm38, please visit the HPComm development page.
