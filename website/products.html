Cables? Software? Here's where to go...

Software

If you'd like HPComm on CD, we recommend purchasing the HP Graphing Calculator PC Connectivity Kit. Here's what you get:

  • The HP version of HPComm. [Check here for upgrades]
  • A complete snapshot of Eric Rechlin's hpcalc.org, dated 2-Aug-99.
  • An all-singing, all-dancing animated demonstration of HP's HP49G graphing calculator.
  • A quality 9-pin to 10-pin serial cable, suitable for the HP38G and HP49G.
  • A 10-pin to 4-pin converter for attaching to HP48-series calculators.
  • HP warranty and support.

Cables

Cables may be obtained from a number of places:
  • Supplied with the HP Graphing Calculator PC Connectivity Kit. See the above note.
  • Samson Cables.
  • CalcPro.
  • Or you could make one yourself using Enrico Carta's fine instructions.

Note, do NOT use an F1015/F1016/200LX cable with your HP38G or HP49G, as they are not compatible, and may damage your calculator. See the HPComm FAQ for more information.
